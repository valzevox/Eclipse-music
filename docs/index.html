Eclipse

Modern Desktop Music Application for Windows

About Eclipse

Eclipse is a desktop music player designed for Windows that lets users organize playlists, stream audio, view synchronized lyrics, and connect their Google/YouTube account to import personal playlists and liked tracks seamlessly.

Key Features

  • Google & YouTube Account Integration: Read-only OAuth 2.0 integration to view and import your YouTube playlists and liked music videos into your local library.
  • Multi-Source Playback & Playlist Management: Organize local tracks and online playlists in one unified desktop interface.
  • Synchronized Lyrics & Audio Controls: Real-time synced lyrics, equalizer, SponsorBlock integration, and taskbar media controls.
  • Local Secure Storage: Authentication tokens are encrypted locally on your device using Electron Safe Storage.
