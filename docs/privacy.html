Eclipse – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 31, 2026

1. Introduction

Eclipse ("we", "our", or "the application") is a desktop application developed to provide users with music-related tools and YouTube account integration. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how Eclipse accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when you connect your Google account.

2. Information We Access

When you choose to connect your Google account, Eclipse uses Google OAuth 2.0 to request permission to access the following information:

  • YouTube account, playlists, and liked videos information.
  • YouTube channel profile information.
  • Publicly available or user-authorized playlist items permitted by the requested read-only API scope.

Eclipse requests the following Google OAuth scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly

Eclipse does not request permission to upload, modify, or delete YouTube videos or playlists through this read-only scope.

3. How We Use Google User Data

Google user data accessed by Eclipse is used strictly to provide the application's user-facing features, including:

  • Connecting your Google account at your request.
  • Retrieving and importing your YouTube playlists and liked music tracks into your local Eclipse library.
  • Maintaining your authenticated session locally so you do not need to repeatedly sign in.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, personalized advertising, analytics profiling, or selling user information.

4. Data Storage and Security

Eclipse stores authentication tokens locally on the user's device to maintain the Google connection. Authentication tokens are encrypted using the operating system's secure storage facilities through Electron Safe Storage (safeStorage).

Access tokens are used exclusively to communicate directly from the user's desktop device with Google's authorized APIs. Refresh tokens are used locally to renew access when necessary.

5. Data Sharing

Eclipse does not sell Google user data. Eclipse does not transfer or share Google user data with any third-party servers, analytics services, or external parties except directly with Google APIs as required to fulfill user-initiated requests, or where required by applicable law.

Google processes information according to its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Authentication tokens are retained locally on the user's device only for as long as necessary to maintain the user's connected Google account. Users may disconnect their Google account at any time inside Eclipse (Settings → Log out), which immediately deletes locally stored authentication tokens and cookies.

Users can also revoke Eclipse's Google account access at any time through: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

Eclipse's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

Eclipse is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application functionality or data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
